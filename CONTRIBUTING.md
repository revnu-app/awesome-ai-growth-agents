# Contributing

Thanks for helping founders find the right tools. Submit through the [issue form](https://github.com/revnu-app/awesome-ai-growth-agents/issues/new?template=submit-a-tool.yml) or open a pull request.

## Rules

- **One tool per submission.**
- **It must be live and usable today.** No waitlists, no "coming soon".
- **Put it in the right section, in alphabetical order.**
- **Format:** `- [Name](https://link) - One neutral sentence ending in a period.`
- **Keep the description neutral and factual,** under 120 characters. No "best", "leading", "#1", emoji or marketing claims.
- **No affiliate or tracking links** on submitted tools.
- **Open-source entries** need a public repository with a commit in the last six months.

Maintainers review every submission and decide what fits the list. Entries that stop working are removed by the weekly link check.
